# P101 generic centrifugal pump: demonstration maintenance manual

Original fictional guidance written for this repository. All intervals, quantities and parts
below are examples, not instructions for a real pump. Use the actual machine documentation.

## Vibration and bearing wear
Rising vibration can indicate bearing wear; inspect the bearing before continued operation.
Example vibration warning is 4.5 mm/s and alarm is 7.1 mm/s. Confirm mounting and measurement
conditions. Check bearing noise, lubricant condition and temperature together with the trend.

## Bearing temperature
Rising bearing temperature can indicate insufficient lubrication or bearing damage.
Example warning is 70 °C and alarm is 85 °C. Verify the temperature sensor and cooling conditions.
Arrange a controlled stop if the alarm persists and follow the site operating procedure.

## Lubrication
Check lubricant condition before adding grease to a warm or vibrating bearing.
For this fictional example only, inspect every 500 operating hours and use 8 g of compatible
grease after cleaning the grease point. Actual intervals, lubricant and quantity must come
from the installed bearing and pump documentation; overgreasing can increase temperature.

## Bearing replacement and lock-out/tag-out
Stop the pump and apply the site's lock-out/tag-out procedure before bearing replacement.
Isolate electrical and process energy, depressurize, drain safely and verify zero energy.
Qualified personnel remove the coupling guard and housing, inspect shaft and seats, replace
the bearing with the specified fit and restore lubrication. Reassemble, align, replace guards,
then release isolation under the site procedure and record post-start vibration and temperature.

## Alignment and misalignment
Check shaft alignment when vibration increases after coupling or foundation work.
With energy isolated, check soft foot and coupling condition. Correct alignment using the
machine's specified tolerances; recheck after thermal stabilization where required.

## Imbalance
Inspect the impeller for deposits or damage if vibration rises with rotational speed.
Isolate and drain the pump before inspection. Have qualified personnel assess balancing.

## Cavitation
Check suction conditions when vibration is accompanied by crackling noise and unstable pressure.
Inspect suction restrictions, liquid level and operating point under the approved procedure.
Do not continue operation outside the pump's permitted range.

## Low discharge pressure
Low discharge pressure below the example 4.0 bar limit requires a process and instrument check.
Check the pressure sensor, liquid supply, suction strainer, valve positions, leaks and impeller
condition. Check for cavitation before assuming a mechanical bearing fault.
